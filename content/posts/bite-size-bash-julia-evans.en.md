## Book Review : Bite-Size Bash by Julia Evans

**Author :** Julia Evans  
**Genre :** Computer Science / Shell  
**Rating :** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (4/5)

### About the book

*Bite-Size Bash* is a practical guide that rounds up the most useful everyday bash commands, explained simply and with concrete examples. Julia Evans is known for her engineering zines (Wizard Zines) and her ability to make technical topics accessible.

### What I liked

- **Clear explanations** : every command is explained in detail, with *why* it works, not just *how*.
- **Made for beginners and seasoned users alike** : you always learn something, even with shell experience.
- **Bite-size format** : perfect for picking up tips between dev sessions without drowning.
- **Concrete examples** : the commands are directly testable and useful in a real workflow.
- **The drawings** : Julia Evans' illustrations are perfect! They completely break away from the monochrome routine of other technical articles and books — think No Starch Press editions — with color and whimsy from the very start, all while staying professional and accessible.

### What I liked less

- We could have done with an **even longer** book once you get a taste of it!
- Some sections remain **skipped over** for advanced users.

### Verdict

An excellent read for **anyone using a terminal on a daily basis**. Quick to read, easy to remember, and directly applicable. A great starting point to boost your command-line productivity.

**Recommended if :** you want to tame the shell with clear, no-nonsense explanations.
