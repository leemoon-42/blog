## Book Review : HTTP : Learn Your Browser's Language by Julia Evans

**Author :** Julia Evans  
**Genre :** Computer Science / Web  
**Rating :** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (4/5)

### About the book

*HTTP : Learn Your Browser's Language* demystifies the protocol we all use every day without thinking about it : HTTP. Requests, responses, headers, caching, cookies... everything is told from the browser's point of view, with Julia Evans' well-known pedagogy.

### What I liked

- **Clear explanations** : finally a book that explains HTTP as a language your browser speaks fluently.
- **Made for beginners and seasoned users alike** : even experienced devs learn details they forgot or misunderstood.
- **Bite-size format** : ideal for picking up a concept between two website debugging sessions.
- **Concrete examples** : every concept is illustrated with real requests, testable with curl or the DevTools.
- **The drawings** : Julia Evans' illustrations are perfect! They completely break away from the monochrome routine of other technical articles and books — think No Starch Press editions — with color and whimsy from the very start, all while staying professional and accessible.

### What I liked less

- We could have done with an **even longer** book once you get a taste of it!
- The most advanced topics (HTTP/2, HTTP/3) remain **skipped over** for the most advanced readers.

### Verdict

The perfect book to **understand what really happens between your browser and the server**. Best read with a terminal nearby to test everything live.

**Recommended if :** you develop for the web, you debug API calls, or you just want to know what the `200` in "HTTP 200 OK" means.
