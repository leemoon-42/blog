## My hobbies

### 🎵 Music

Music keeps me company when I code, when I work in the lab, or just to relax. My tastes:

- **Instruments and rhythmic music**: I love the style of **Nujabes**, especially his **Hexalogy** with **Shing02**, the **Samurai Champloo OSTs**, as well as the instrumental compositions of **Mark Sparling** (A Short Hike), **Christopher Larkin** (Hollow Knight) and **Toby Fox** (Undertale / Deltarune)
- **French and Belgian rap**: I listen to a lot of **Nekfeu**, **Vald**, **Orelsan**, **Luidji**, **Romeo Elvis**, **JeanJass** or **Caballero**
- **US rap and big American sounds**: **Imagine Dragons**, **Maroon 5**, **OneRepublic** or **Twenty One Pilots**
- **J-pop / K-pop**: I also love Japanese and Korean sounds — **LISA**, **YUI**, **FLOW**, **Creepy Nuts**, etc.
- **Rock and classics**: legendary bands like **The Beatles**, **Queen**, **AC/DC** or **Survivor**
- **Anime openings**: I'm a fan of openings, especially those from the legendary **JoJo's Bizarre Adventure** by **Hirohiko Araki**.

### 🎮 Video games

Video games are my favorite break after a long working session. I love story-driven universes as much as challenges that make you think. My favorite genres:

- **FPS**: fast-paced battles like **Call of Duty** or **Counter-Strike**
- **Metroidvania**: exploration and hidden worlds like **Hollow Knight**
- **Soulslike**: the demanding challenge and the satisfaction of victory, with classics like **Dark Souls** or **Elden Ring**
- **Roguelike**: runs to replay and endless replayability, like **Dead Cells** or **Hades**
- **Arcade games**: for the instant fun and competitive side, like **NSUNS4** (Naruto Shippuden: Ultimate Ninja Storm 4) or **Brawlhalla**
- **Retro / handhelds**: classics on old **Sony** and **Nintendo** handhelds — **PSP**, **PS Vita**, **Nintendo 3DS** — and I really enjoy **modding** and **homebrew**
